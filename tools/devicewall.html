The same route at four real viewport widths, at once. Each frame is a genuine browsing context, so innerWidth, media queries, dvh and clamp() all resolve the way they will on the device — which is not true of headless --window-size, which lays out at a default width and crops the screenshot to the size you asked for. That discrepancy is what makes a layout look broken on a phone when it is not, and look fine when it is broken.
